Fix header controls overlapping on narrow screens. With many controls on (tabs, a version selector, the language switcher, search, and the icon buttons), the version and language pills squeezed on top of each other on phones, and the tab bar ran into them on tablets. Below the desktop breakpoint, selectors, the version selector, and the language switcher now sit at the top of the navigation drawer, full width. The remaining header controls keep their size, and only the site title truncates to make room.
