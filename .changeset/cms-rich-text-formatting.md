Sanity bold and italic spans keep their edge spaces outside the markers, so `Hello ` in bold before `world` renders as bold text instead of literal asterisks, and nested list items indent to their parent item's text, so an item under a numbered one nests instead of joining the parent's line. Notion video captions show `<` and `>` as typed rather than as `&lt;` and `&gt;`, and a rate-limited Notion request waits for the `Retry-After` the API sends.
