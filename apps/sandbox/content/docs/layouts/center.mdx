title: Center
description: "`mode: center` drops the sidebar and the table of contents, and centers a wider column."
mode: center
---

A centered page reads top to bottom with nothing beside it: release notes, a long announcement, a changelog. The header stays, and the page-end links and footer follow the content as usual.

## Why center

With no sidebar or outline pulling the eye sideways, the reader stays with the text. The column is wider than a docs page's but still a comfortable measure.
